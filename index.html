01 / About

About

Final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at College of Engineering Guindy, Anna University, specializing in digital VLSI design. My work covers RTL design in Verilog and SystemVerilog, functional verification, and RTL-to-GDSII implementation using Yosys, OpenLane, and OpenROAD on SKY130A.

I have designed a 5-stage pipelined RISC-V processor core, implemented L1 cache replacement policies during an internship with the SHAKTI Processor Group at IIT Madras, and taken digital designs through to GDSII, including a Tiny Tapeout asynchronous FIFO.

My interests are computer architecture, processor design, and physical implementation.

02 / Featured Project

Featured Project

Team project Jul 2026 – Present Role: Design Lead

5-Stage Pipelined RV32I Processor Core with Hardware Multiplier

Designed the RTL architecture, datapath, and control logic of a 32-bit RISC-V processor in SystemVerilog, starting from a single-cycle baseline and extending it to a 5-stage pipeline (IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB). The hardware multiplier, developed by a teammate, is integrated into the pipeline. Wrote SystemVerilog testbenches and verified instruction execution, branching, and memory operations in Vivado simulation. The pipelined core is functionally working with the multiplier integrated.
